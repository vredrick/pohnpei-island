# Places to Stay: source ledger

Reviewed 25 September 2026. Scope: all ten properties in the Pohnpei Tourism Office accommodation directory. Local preview only; no deployment or business outreach. Existing images are unchanged; this page previously had no images and remains text-led.

## Source hierarchy

- [Pohnpei Tourism Office directory](https://visitpohnpei.travel/accommodations-and-lodging/): names, area labels, primary phone/email, and initial website discovery. All ten entries are included, alphabetically, without ranking or endorsement.
- Property-owned websites: original summaries of published room details. No visitor review is used as evidence for amenities.
- [WCPFC Circular 2025/40, 26 June 2025](https://meetings.wcpfc.int/file/17549/download), printed pages 4–6: supplementary room types and amenities for seven properties, explicitly labeled **2025 information · please reconfirm**. The PDF's date, not the search engine's misleading relative publication date, is authoritative. Table columns were visually inspected. We omit its rates, room inventory counts, named staff, and transfer prices.
- [Tourism contact](https://visitpohnpei.travel/contact-us/) and [getting here](https://visitpohnpei.travel/getting-to-pohnpei/): onward links, not a reproduction of flight/entry guidance.

The review date indicates a source check, not a property inspection or a new confirmation from an owner. Filter matches include the dated information; absence from a feature filter means the feature was not established by our selected sources. No live availability, rate comparisons, quality ratings, or booking engine is implied.

Other properties in the WCPFC sheet (East Wing, Joy, Beacon Towers, AA Getaway) are outside this release's official-directory inventory. The page explicitly avoids claiming exhaustive coverage and directs visitors to tourism for other accommodation and outer-island arrangements.

## Excluded or conflicting sources

A fetched visit-micronesia.fm accommodation index returned unrelated gambling content even though search snippets described tourism. It is not used or linked. Historic brochures, third-party reviews, and aggregator availability are not treated as current property confirmation. Unavailable domains and blocked social pages are logged above rather than presented as working visitor links.

## Editorial decisions

Removed unsupported generic claims about chain resorts, universal amenities, hotels filling fast, and unspecified Airbnb stock. Replaced the disabled finder with name, area, and published-feature filters. No-JavaScript visitors see every listing and contact. Booking questions are original planning guidance, not claims that services are included. Accommodation and Nan Madol guides link in both directions.

## Maintenance

Recheck directory contacts and property links before release and at least quarterly. Replace dated room details when an owner or newer authoritative publication confirms them; update provenance and filter tags together. Change review dates only after an actual review. Verify disputed location pins before adding a map. Keep rates and availability with the property until a maintained source and clear update process exist.
