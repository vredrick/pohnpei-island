# Adventure guides: source audit and editorial decisions

Reviewed internally: 26 September 2026. Covers `/waterfalls-and-jungle`, `/diving-and-surfing`, `/outer-atolls`, their homepage/planning entry points, and related shared operator links. User authorized all three PRD tasks and specifically requested Pohnpei Eco-Adventure Guide as an audit source. This is a review of published sources, not field verification or confirmation from businesses. No images were added, removed or altered.

## Source approach

Use original descriptions with links beside the relevant content. Pohnpei Eco-Adventure Guide supplies detailed place context; the Pohnpei Tourism Office remains the baseline for current published business contacts. A recent retrieval or a 2026 copyright footer does not establish when an article's practical details were last verified. Several place articles were published in 2013–2014; the Ahnd/Pakin article is dated 2021. Omit old prices, schedules, route timings and contact details where current confirmation is absent. Keep the review date internal.

The tourism office's [adventure page](https://visitpohnpei.travel/adventure/) itself links to Eco-Adventure destination guides. Its month-by-month suggestions are promotional ideas, not forecasts or operating schedules. They do not settle seasonal conflicts. The Eco-Adventure homepage also carries a July 2023 travel notice; it is not used for current entry requirements.

## New waterfall guide

Four selected routes have individual sources, terrain context and questions to resolve. This is a useful starting set, not an exhaustive trail inventory. The Kepirohi warning is clearly attributed to the guide rather than represented as a current water-quality test. The page suggests a viewing visit. Permission, fees, condition and transport questions are editorial planning prompts; no fixed opening hours, prices, guide prices or durations have been invented.

The homepage's waterfall card now opens the actual route and drops unsupported waterfall/rainfall totals and whole-island-view claims. Its original `#adventures` anchor remains. The ocean card identifies P-Pass as Palikir Pass and removes its all-weather access promise. Other homepage content remains outside this focused audit.

## Outer-island audit

Removed the “time left alone” framing, blanket “visitors welcome” assurance, absolute Ahnd uninhabited claim, fixed boat times and emphasis on Pingelap's medical/genetic history. Added hosting, consent, transport, provisions and return questions. Farther-island coverage now includes Sapwuahfik and distinguishes the sea journeys of Nukuoro/Kapingamarangi from possible small-aircraft destinations. Oroluk/Minto are inquiries requiring specialized arrangements. Names follow the linked sources; variants are given where helpful, not presented as new official naming decisions.

No current ship operator was independently verified for a specific farther island. Refer readers to official tourism for an introduction. Caroline Islands Air is a published outer-atoll-flight contact, not proof of a route to every island on the page. No flight link is assigned to Nukuoro or Kapingamarangi.

## Operator relationship audit

- Shared `operators.ts` remains the only contact dataset. All nine entries and six Nan Madol relationships are preserved.
- Hiking contacts use existing published waterfall/hiking categories; no specific trail-to-business relationship is inferred.
- Dive/ocean contacts use the published ocean category; this does not assert all businesses provide SCUBA or all listed sites. Each row retains its actual service description.
- Only Surf Club gains new destination relationships: surfing/P-Pass from the surfing guide and Ahnd from the explicit atoll-guide referral. These are published connections that require reconfirmation. No Pakin relationship is inferred.
- Existing service filters remain based on the official directory. No surfing category is inferred merely from a business name. Surf inquiries have their own contextual link.
- Source labels distinguish Eco-Adventure references from operators' own websites. Directory links return to the relevant guide section.
- Ocean Cruise remains visibly paused in the directory and every activity listing where it appears. No phone, email or WhatsApp contact was changed.
