## Conflicts and editorial decisions

- **Nauru Airlines:** the published [8–30 September 2026 timetable](https://www.nauruair.com/library/files/20260902ns26passengerflightschedule-08sep-30sep2026updated.pdf) omits PNI. The [1 October 2026–2 April 2027 timetable](https://www.nauruair.com/library/files/20260902ns26-27passengerflightschedule-01oct2026-02apr2027updated.pdf) includes it. Both documents bear 7 September 2026 publication dates. Public copy explicitly says PNI appears in selected schedule periods and tells readers to verify both directions. Do not describe a current uninterrupted Brisbane–PNI service or suggest it is nonstop. The live schedule index is the public link rather than an expiring PDF.
- **Passport validity:** FSM tourism says 120 days after entry; U.S. State Department says six months after arrival. Pohnpei tourism also advises six months. Public copy explains that guidance differs and routes the traveler to FSM Immigration and their carrier rather than deciding which rule will govern an individual itinerary. This remains a confirmation question for the responsible authority.
- **U.S. visitors:** retain the State Department's visa-free, indefinite-stay provision under COFA. Remove the old unqualified claim of “no special permits” for all work; professional/business requirements are distinct. Preserve the existing `#us-visitors` link target.
- **Departure fee:** Pohnpei tourism quotes approximately USD25; FSM tourism quotes USD20. Omit an exact amount; tell readers to confirm the charge/payment method and retain cash. Local confirmation is still needed before any fixed figure is published.
- **Health and conditions:** remove old claims that the reef is always accessible, a particular season guarantees diving, and Kolonia tap water is generally safe. Do not carry the old hospital-in-Kolonia location claim into the revised guide. Link professional/current advice and prompt visitors to agree on day-specific plans with guides.
- **Potentially stale or unsuitable references:** older FSM Embassy FAQ content corroborates short visits but includes old material; do not use it for current operational details. Exclude the previously observed unrelated content at visit-micronesia.fm. A search result calling evisa-megov.com an “official portal” was not independently authenticated and is not linked or used.
